How it works

A broker, not a chatbot

what it does · what it matches · what it refuses to claim

You type an intent. A worker turns it into tool calls, a broker decides whether they are allowed, and an agent runs the ones that are — all inside this tab. The interesting part is the third step: what an agent is permitted to do, who decides, and what a refusal actually means.
